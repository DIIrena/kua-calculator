# Nano Banana Pro prompts v2 - Nine Life Areas photo plates (2026-07-22)

Knowledge-base grounded rewrite of [image-prompts-nine-areas-2026-07-21.md](image-prompts-nine-areas-2026-07-21.md).
The old file is kept untouched for comparison. Same twelve plates, same filenames,
same export settings (JPEG, quality ~70-80, longest edge ~1600px, <= 250KB each,
<= 2.5MB total); approved files still go into `content/photos/` named exactly as
headed below.

Restyled 2026-07-22 (v2.1) toward the owner's art direction, taken from her own
better-performing cover prompt and generation:

- Walls are rough textured ivory plaster with raw natural texture, never flat paint.
- Wall art is a feature: one huge wordless abstract expressionist oil painting in
  premium muted colours (palette chosen per plate to suit the sector's element)
  and, or, grids of small wordless abstract single-line drawings in slim pale oak
  frames. Always wordless, faceless, unsigned.
- Juxtaposition is the core styling idea: minimalism against expressionism,
  japanese origami-like folded simplicity against raw textures, one modern
  industrial designed piece (sculptural oak or brass lamp) against soft natural
  materials, big screen cinematic composition.
- Textiles are tactile and fluffy: velvet upholstery, fluffy ivory wool throws,
  deep-pile ivory rugs, chunky weaves, set against raw plaster and honest grain.
- Air: every room reads tall, open and breathing, light visibly moving through.
- Finish: soft cinematic haze and a touch of HDR depth are now welcome. The
  negatives "no oversaturation", "no lens flare", "no cinematic haze" and
  "no HDR gloss" were removed everywhere; "no neon colours" stays.

What v2 already carried and keeps: every prompt is grounded in the audited
chapter library at `projects/feng-shui/content/`. Placement rules survive
untouched: command position, Azure Dragon and White Tiger flanking, door-based
corner positions, compass-matched light direction, exact object counts and
pairings, filled-never-empty vessels, one-anchored-cure restraint, and every
deliberate absence (no mirror here, no water there, no fire in the northwest).
Negative prompts still block the mistakes the knowledge base warns against.

How to use: copy only the prompt paragraph into the generator. The
"Why this styling" bullets under each prompt are review notes for you, not
prompt text.

Aspect ratios: cover is portrait 4:5; all others landscape 3:2. Set the
ratio in the generator where possible; otherwise generate large and crop.
