## The shape in one glance

- **~44 buyable products + 2 free tools.**
- Only **10 are on the shelf** (●). The other **~34 are live but hidden** (○) - reachable by direct link, but not shown in the shop, search, or sitemap.
- The biggest hidden block is the **21 single-topic Compasses** ($7 each): 12 rooms + 9 life areas. Each is a slice of a bundle you already sell:
  - the 12 rooms **are** the Twelve Spaces Compass ($29),
  - the 9 areas **are** the Nine Life Areas Compass ($29).

## How this connects to the ladder plan

The **●** products are exactly the ladder-led shop from the other plan. The **○**
catalogue is the *deep inventory* behind it. Two honest options for those 34:

1. **Leave them hidden** (current strategy): the shop stays simple; the $7
   singles exist mainly as low-price entry points for paid ads or specific
   searches ("bedroom feng shui").
2. **Add a "browse the full catalogue" door**: one link at the bottom of the
   shop that opens a "by room / by life area" grid of the singles, for the
   shopper who wants to buy just one $7 room rather than the $29 bundle. This
   is where a second, smaller comparison table could live ("one room $7 vs.
   all twelve $29").
