# The product funnel (2026-08-14)

The owner's question: which product goes first in the funnel? Her instinct: the
Personal BaZi Reading. Verdict: **the instinct is right, with one refinement.**
The first thing a stranger meets is never a paid product; it is the free BaZi
calculator. The Reading is the first thing they BUY. And the funnel must not
end there: the Reading is the doorway into the home catalogue, where the higher
prices live.

## Why the person funnel leads (the strategic argument)

1. **The market is bigger where the mirror is.** Self-discovery content
   (birth charts, personality, "what your birth date says about you") massively
   outperforms home-advice content on short video. People share things that are
   about THEM. The BaZi calculator is a mirror; the Kua calculator is a tool.
   Mirrors travel further on social.
2. **The BaZi calculator captures; the Kua calculator cannot.** The Kua
   calculator is anonymous by design (privacy-pure, a hard platform rule). The
   BaZi calculator requires sign-in and saves the chart, so every single user
   becomes an email address plus a stored chart. It is the only top-of-funnel
   surface that builds the list by itself.
3. **The Reading is the perfect first sale.** Fully personalised (feels made
   for you, because it is), rendered by code at zero marginal cost, delivered
   instantly, and priced at the impulse threshold. A 32-page book about
   yourself for $19 is the strongest value story in the catalogue.
4. **But the home catalogue is where the money compounds.** The ladder tops at
   $49, the deep catalogue holds ~35 more products, and home improvement is a
   repeat need (rooms, years, moves). So the person funnel ACQUIRES, the home
   catalogue ASCENDS.

One sentence: **BaZi opens the relationship; the home closes it.**

## Stage by stage, with the psychology named

**Stage 0 - Attention.** Short vertical videos from the chapter library.
Identity hooks route to /bazi-calculator, home hooks to /good-days or
/kua-calculator. Principle: self-relevance + mere exposure (consistent brand
presence across channels).

**Stage 1 - The free gift.** The calculators give a real, generous result, not
a teaser stub. Principles: zero-price effect (free is categorically different),
reciprocity (give first, plainly), authority (the result looks like it was
drawn by a master, because our visual language is that good).

**Stage 2 - Capture.** BaZi door: sign-in to run, chart saved to the account.
Home door: the Good-Days catcher. Principles: commitment and consistency (a
saved chart is a small commitment that makes the next step natural), endowment
(it is THEIR chart now; people do not abandon what they own), IKEA effect (they
typed in their own birth moment; the result is partly theirs).

**Stage 3 - The first sale: Personal BaZi Reading, $19.** The free results card
shows the four pillars and the Day Master fully, then names what the Reading
adds (your season, your chart's strength, your decades road, your helpful
people) as visible, titled, locked sections. Principle: Zeigarnik (an open loop
the Reading closes), hyperbolic discounting (delivered in minutes, emphasize
the immediacy), goal-gradient ("your chart is drawn; the reading is the second
half"). Price at $19: matches the ladder entry rung, under the impulse
threshold, and a 32-page personalised book at $19 is an obvious-value frame
(contrast effect against the $49 anchor shown beside it).

**Stage 4 - The bridge (person to home).** The Reading's closing pages and its
delivery email both say one thing: "BaZi reads the person. Feng shui reads the
place the person lives." with ONE call: the Personal Feng Shui Compass $19, and
the Complete Home Compass $49 named beside it as the anchor. Principles:
commitment and consistency (a second $19 yes is easy after the first),
anchoring and good-better-best (the ladder table does the selling), paradox of
choice (never show the 45-product catalogue; show one next step).

**Stage 5 - Deepening.** Move-In Report and 7-Day Reset surface only on their
triggers (moving, overwhelm) in the sequence and on the shop's "For a Moment"
row. Kits ride as add-ons and delivery-email postscripts. The hidden $7 singles
stay hidden from the shelf and work as side doors for specific searches and
future ads, each upselling to its $29 bundle on-page. Principles: framing,
rule of 100 (percentage framing beats absolute at these prices), contrast
("one room $7, all twelve $29").

**Stage 6 - Retention.** The newsletter plus the yearly rhythm: Year Ahead
Compass, the 2027 Planner and calendar editions, and (future, after the Reading
proves itself) an annual "your year in your chart" BaZi letter, which would be
the catalogue's first true yearly re-purchase about the person. Principles:
peak-end (the certificate page and a warm delivery email are the peak; the
review ask rides that moment), feedback loops (buyers leave reviews, reviews
lift conversion, the flywheel turns).

## What we deliberately do NOT do

- **No fake scarcity, no countdown timers.** The brand is calm authority;
  manufactured urgency would spend trust we cannot buy back. The only deadlines
  we speak are real ones (annual editions genuinely expire).
- **No discount training.** EARLYLIST-style codes stay for list moments, not a
  permanent banner. Second-order effect: constant discounts teach waiting.
- **No catalogue dumps.** One CTA per email, one question per page. The shelf
  stays curated at 10.
- **BaZi Basics ($14) does not compete with the Reading.** Repositioned as the
  "learn to read charts yourself" companion: cross-sold AFTER the Reading (the
  delivery email's postscript), never beside it (Hick's law: two similar offers
  at the same moment kill both).

## Measurement (the four numbers that matter)

1. Door sessions (person door vs home door, by channel UTM).
2. Capture rate (sessions → emails: sign-ins + good-days).
3. First-purchase rate (emails → Reading or ladder-entry sales).
4. Ascension rate (first buyers → any second purchase inside 30 days).

Weekly in the funnel readout; the monthly review doubles down on whichever door
converts better end to end, not on raw sessions.
