# Nano Banana Pro prompts - Product cover plates (2026-07-24)

Per-product magazine covers for the four compass products that do not
already have their own cover. Same knowledge-grounded, owner-art-direction
style as [image-prompts-nine-areas-v2-2026-07-22.md](image-prompts-nine-areas-v2-2026-07-22.md).

Each is a different room and moment, like different issues of one
magazine, but all share the cover treatment: the photo bleeds full-page,
a huge ivory Didone masthead reading COMPASS overlays the top, the orange
brand mark sits above it, and the personalised title lines sit over the
bottom under a soft deep-green scrim. So each prompt keeps the TOP of the
frame calm and mid-toned (wall art, plaster, shadow) for the masthead,
and the LOWER FIFTH simple for the title.

Filenames matter: the PDF template looks for `cover-<product-slug>.jpg`
first and falls back to the shared `cover.jpg`. Drop an approved file into
`content/photos/` under the exact name headed below and that product's
cover lights up automatically. Ratio: portrait 4:5 (same as the shared
cover). Export settings unchanged (JPEG, longest edge ~1600px, aim ~200KB
each so the folder stays under its 2.5MB cap).

Owner art direction baked into every prompt: rough textured ivory plaster
walls; wall art as a feature (wordless, faceless, unsigned); the mandatory
origami note and floor-length sheer linen curtains; juxtaposition of
minimalism and expressionism; one industrial designed piece; fluffy
textiles against raw textures; airy breathing composition; soft cinematic
haze and a touch of HDR depth in the finish. No people, no readable text,
ever. Feng shui grounding is per-scene: command position, flanking, room
placements, deliberate absences.

How to use: copy only the prompt paragraph into the generator. The "Why
this styling" bullets are review notes, not prompt text.
