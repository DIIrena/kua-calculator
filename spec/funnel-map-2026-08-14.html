What this funnel never does

No fake scarcity or countdown timers. No permanent discounts that train waiting. No catalogue dumps: one CTA per email, one question per page, ten products on the shelf. BaZi Basics ($14) is cross-sold after the Reading, never beside it.

Build list to switch it on

  1. Finish the Reading visuals (owner: two icon sheets; Claude: wire the template)
  2. Register the Reading: commerce + product page + Stripe price
  3. Shelf: one-card "About you" row beside the ladder
  4. Locked-chapter tease + one CTA on the calculator result
  5. Bridge spread in the PDF + delivery-email copy
  6. BaZi branch of the welcome sequence
  7. UTM scheme + funnel-readout stage columns
