# Nano Banana Pro prompts - Twelve Spaces room plates (2026-07-24)

Twelve room chapter plates for the Twelve Spaces Compass (and the room
chapters inside the Complete Home Compass). Same knowledge-grounded,
owner-art-direction style as the nine-areas file.

Filenames match the block IDs in `lib/products.ts` exactly, so the PDF
chapter openers pick each plate up automatically when you drop an approved
JPEG into `content/photos/` under the name headed below. Ratio: landscape
3:2 for all twelve.

Budget note: the Complete Home Compass is already ~3.9MB and renders these
same twelve plates plus the nine area plates. To stay under Vercel's 4.5MB
response limit, keep these room plates SMALL on export, aim ~130KB each
(longest edge ~1500px, JPEG quality ~70). The 2.5MB folder cap in
scripts/smoke-blocks.mjs counts every JPEG in content/photos, so the room
set plus the existing plates will need the cap raised or the plates kept
lean; flag this before shipping the full set.

Owner art direction baked into every prompt: rough textured ivory plaster
walls; wall art as a feature (wordless, faceless, unsigned) where the room
allows; the mandatory origami note and floor-length sheer linen curtains
(windowless rooms keep the origami and explain how daylight and air still
enter); juxtaposition of minimalism and expressionism; one industrial
designed piece; fluffy textiles against raw textures; airy breathing
composition; soft cinematic haze and a touch of HDR depth. Each scene is
unmistakably its own room, grounded in the room-by-room and diagnostic
chapters of the knowledge base. No people, no readable text, ever.

How to use: copy only the prompt paragraph into the generator. The "Why
this styling" bullets are review notes, not prompt text.
